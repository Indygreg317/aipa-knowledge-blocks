# Carbon and Sustainability Examples

This directory is reserved for AIPA Knowledge Block examples focused on carbon management, sustainability reporting, emissions methodology, supplier data review, evidence validation, and audit preparation.

AIPA public material is available at [aipa.network](https://aipa.network).

---

## Purpose

Carbon and sustainability examples show how Knowledge Blocks can structure environmental reporting decisions and methodology-dependent workflows.

These examples should demonstrate how sustainability knowledge can be:

- represented with clear methodology references
- supported by reviewable evidence
- versioned when reporting rules or methods change
- validated before reporting use
- revalidated when evidence becomes stale
- audited for traceability

---

## Example Concepts

Future examples may include:

- emissions calculation methodology check
- supplier data completeness review
- reporting evidence validation
- methodology version control
- audit preparation workflow
- sustainability claim review
- carbon data exception handling

---

## Suggested Controls

Carbon and sustainability Knowledge Blocks often require:

- evidence freshness windows
- methodology version tracking
- verification status checks
- policy or standard consistency checks
- supersession when reporting methods change
- audit log required
- human review for public claims or regulated reporting

---

## Implementation Boundary

These examples are reference artifacts only. Real carbon, sustainability, ESG, or environmental reporting use may require qualified methodology, accounting, legal, regulatory, or assurance review.

A Knowledge Block can structure the workflow, but it does not replace required professional or regulatory review.
