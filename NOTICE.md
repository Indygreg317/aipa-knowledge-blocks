## Reference Materials

The schemas, templates, examples, and documentation in this repository are reference artifacts.

They are intended to support learning, design, implementation planning, governance review, and structured experimentation around governed AI systems.

They should not be treated as finished production policies, legal advice, compliance advice, security advice, safety advice, financial advice, environmental reporting advice, or operational approval.

## Deployment Responsibility

Users and organizations are responsible for reviewing and adapting these materials before using them in real systems.

Before deployment, users should confirm:

- domain expert review
- legal or regulatory review where applicable
- security and privacy review where applicable
- compliance review where applicable
- safety review where applicable
- schema validation
- evidence quality and freshness
- authority requirements
- runtime controls
- audit requirements
- human oversight and escalation paths

AIPA Knowledge Blocks provide structure. They do not replace organizational accountability or qualified professional review.

## Governance Boundary

A Knowledge Block should not execute merely because it exists, was retrieved, or was previously approved.

The intended operating rule is:

> Structured knowledge should not become action unless it remains governed, current, authorized, and auditable.

Runtime use should distinguish:

- structural validity
- trust level
- verification status
- approval
- invocation
- execution authorization
- audit record
